Documents

Everything you expect from a word processor

  • Paginated editing with styles, multilevel lists, columns, sections and watermarks.
  • Tables, images with text wrapping, shapes, equations and editable charts.
  • Tables of contents, footnotes, cross-references, citations and bibliography.
  • Track changes, threaded comments, document compare and spelling.
  • Mail merge, labels and envelopes; export to PDF, HTML and EPUB.

Spreadsheets

The formulas and charts you use every day

  • About 370 functions, including Excel’s newer ones: FILTER, SORT, UNIQUE…
  • Styled tables, pivot tables, filters, validation and conditional formatting.
  • Column, line, pie, waterfall, histogram, radar charts and more.
  • Goal seek, scenarios, Solver and forecast sheets.
  • Projects inside the sheet: Gantt, critical path, resources and costs.

Presentations

Design, rehearse and present

  • Themes, masters and slide layouts; slide sorter and Outline view.
  • Transitions, including one that morphs a slide into the next, and animations.
  • Presenter view with notes, clock and next slide; pen and laser pointer.
  • Video and audio, tables, charts, equations and editable diagrams.
  • Export to PDF, to images and to audience handouts.

PDF

Work with PDF without leaving the program

  • Thumbnails, bookmarks, search and presentation mode.
  • Highlights, notes, text boxes, freehand drawing, shapes and stamps.
  • Fill in forms and sign, including with a digital certificate (PAdES).
  • Real redaction: what you hide is removed from the file, not just covered.
  • Merge, split and compare, or turn a PDF into an editable document.
  • .pdf
  • PDF/A
Databases

Related tables, queries, forms and reports

  • Build your tables (customers, orders, invoices) and link them together.
  • See each customer’s orders at a glance, on the same screen.
  • Search and summarise your data by dragging fields, no code needed (or in SQL, if you prefer).
  • Forms and reports with wizards, grouping and totals.
  • Imports Access and dBase; connects to PostgreSQL, MySQL and SQL Server.
  • SQLite
  • .accdb
  • .mdb
  • .dbf
Notebooks

Notes that link to each other

  • Sections and pages with rich text, tables, pictures and files.
  • Links between pages with [[, backlinks and a graph of the whole notebook.
  • Tasks from every page in one list, or as a board with your own columns.
  • Canvas pages, a timeline, page history and password-locked sections.
  • Imports Obsidian vaults; exports to Markdown, DOCX and PDF.
  • .apexnote
  • .md
